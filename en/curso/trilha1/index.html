🛠️ Use AI as a craft

The 7 habits that turn AI from a “curious toy” into a work tool. From “AI isn’t just for techies” to “building professional and financial security.”

0 of 7 0%
What is:

Using AI to your advantage doesn't require programming. Writing, sales, planning, and customer service are already AI territory.

Why learn:

People who apply AI to real tasks in their own field get ahead of those waiting for "someone in IT" to solve it.

Key concepts:

Domain > code · real task > demo · initiative > waiting.

What is:

Apply AI to concrete day-to-day problems until it becomes the team’s go-to practical resource.

Why learn:

It's not about knowing everything — it's about solving problems with AI before others do, building professional capital.

Key concepts:

Solve > explain · small wins · share what works.

What is:

Review it, adapt it to your style, and decide whether the result deserves your name.

Why learn:

AI generates convincing answers—not always correct ones. The judgment is yours.

Key concepts:

Convincing ≠ correct · check the "ground truth" · you sign off.

What is:

Feed AI real information—your projects, priorities, examples, and patterns.

Why learn:

Good context is what separates a mediocre answer from a tailored one. It’s the biggest lever.

Key concepts:

Real data > generic prompt · examples > vague instructions · your work’s standards.

What is:

Move fast, test, fix, and improve—instead of trying to get everything right the first time.

Why learn:

Speed comes from the short cycle, not from initial perfection. You improve by seeing the result.

Key concepts:

Draft > perfection · short cycle · feedback becomes a correction.

What is:

Identify what’s repetitive and predictable, then automate it—sometimes without AI at all.

Why learn:

Simple automation (fixed rule) is cheaper, more reliable, and easier to audit than AI when the task is deterministic.

Key concepts:

Predictable → rule · ambiguous → AI · lower cost, lower risk.

What is:

Use AI to build new sources of income around a field you know well.

Why learn:

Reduces dependence on a single job and turns productivity into a career advantage.

Key concepts:

Multiple income streams · your own asset · productivity becomes optional.
